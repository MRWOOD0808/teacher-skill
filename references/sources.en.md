## Interpretation boundary

“Author’s historical claim” summarizes the argument of an essay in its historical context. “Synthesis grounded in the text” combines a method across essays. “Modern transfer” adds rights protection, evidence grading, reversible pilots, compliance, and professional boundaries. These layers must not be merged.

Some essays contain warfare, repression, class struggle, and coercive historical policy. The skill does not abstract those means into general management techniques and never substitutes authorial authority for current law, ethics, scientific evidence, or professional standards.
